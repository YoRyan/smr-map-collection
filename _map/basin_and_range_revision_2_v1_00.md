  Ever wonder what it would be like to run a real railroad? This scenario will give you command of two Railways: the Atchison, Topeka and Santa Fe Railway from 1950, and the Burlington Northern Railway starting in 1970. Then experience their merger to form the Burlington Northern and Santa Fe Railway in 1997. Take a ride on a Super Chief passenger train, or transport double stack containers with dual AC-4400s. Build the railroad of your dreams with over 25 real-life engine paint schemes from these railways.

  In memory of Big Papa and Choobacca, I thought it would be nice to enlarge the landmap to a size 18. Money has been added and the time has been extended a tad to compensate. All objectives are the same as the size 13 map.
