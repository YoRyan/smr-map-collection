description: 'LOOK AHEAD-LOOK SOUTH.  This map covers the region of the US formerly
  served by the Southern Railroad (Washington to Jacksonville to New Orleans to St.
  Louis).  Formed over 160 years from the consolidation of over 150 rail lines, begining
  in 1830 with The BEST FRIEND OF CHARLESTON - America''s 2nd RR owned by the South
  Carolina Canal and RR Co. A military target of the Union during the 1st Railroad
  War (Civil War) it was reorganized by J P Morgan in 1893 into the Southern RR.  Known
  as a first class modern RR with mottos: LOOK AHEAD - LOOK SOUTH, SOUTHERN SERVES
  THE SOUTH, and SOUTHERN GIVES A GREEN LIGHT TO INNOVATION. See if you can do as
  well as J P Morgan did.  The Victory Scenario should be a challenge to experienced
  players especially with AI players.  The map size is 15 x 15 with 5 Eras from 1918
  to 1985.  Good Luck.'
