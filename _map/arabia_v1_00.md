  During the 1930's the Ghawar oil field, largest in the world, was discovered in eastern Saudi Arabia. Oil wells were developed by ARAMCO (the Arabian American Oil Company) and oil exporting began. Oil is still being pumped from the Ghawar field and accounts for most of Saudi Arabian exports. Kuwait, Qatar, United Arab Emirates, Iraq and Iran also export oil and are included in this map. Build a railroad to help develop the region. In addition to oil you will need to move dates, livestock, milk, and grain grown in the "Wadis", the Oases of the desert. Wadis are valleys where scarce water may be found after rare rainfalls or pumped from wells. Late in the game you will need to move pilgrims to Mecca and Medina, and build some of the rail projects currently planned by the Saudi Arabian Railway Organization.
