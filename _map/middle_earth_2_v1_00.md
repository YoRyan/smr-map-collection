description: |-
  In a world where Orcs, Elves, Humans, and other ethnic groups have long made peace, a rush to make money is bringing large-scale development.  Promote prosperity and political unity by creating a railroad network to link all Kingdoms.

  NOTES
  -   This scenario uses 'slow time', so that each month takes 3.5 times as long as normal.  Engine maintenance costs are adjusted accordingly.
  -   Agricultural industries located in cities produce very little output per month.  After hauling off the initial four loads of output that will have accumulated, make sure to adjust train consists accordingly.
  -   Industries located in the countryside produce faster than those in cities, but slower than in normal games.  Livestock production can be boosted by feed delveries, and grain production by fertilizer deliveries.
  -   First-class passengers are produced and demanded only by Banks.  Boost output by delivering wine and paper.
  -   Second-class passenger output can be boosted by supplying Department Stores with textiles and manufactured goods.
