description: GONE WITH THE WIND...This map covers the Southern United States from
  the introduction of the railroad (1830) to the end of the Civil War (1866).  The
  southern states were rich in agricultural resources, and developed a strong economy
  dependent on slave labor to grow cotton and tobacco which were exported to England
  and the northern states.  The game is intended to be played with 3 AI's (that you
  will need to buy out) while meeting all the objectives given on the "Victory" screen
  tab.  Prepare to use only steam locomotives and replace them as more powerful locomotives
  become available. Run lots of trains to move available goods.  The objectives can
  all be met, but it is not easy. Good Luck.
