description: During World War II Japan melted down nonessential rails and rolling
  stock for military purposes.  This scenario calls for rebuilding the railroad to
  help with development of a post-war Japanese economy based on the import of raw
  materials and export of manufactured goods.  Move passengers, mail, and food around
  the country.  Move raw materials from ports to manufacturing plants and ship export
  goods back to the ports.  Most locomotives are electric due to scarce coal and oil
  resources on the Japanese Islands. Make sure "Newspaper Events" under "Alerts" on
  the Options Menu is "ON" to see useful information during the game.
