description: The Canadian Pacific (CP) was the first transcontinental railroad in
  Canada, running from Thunder Bay in Ontario, across the plains of Manitoba, Saskatchewan,
  and Alberta, then across the Rocky Mountains of British Columbia to Vancouver on
  the west coast of Canada.  The first train to travel the entire route arrived in
  Vancouver on June 30, 1886.  Granted 25 million acres of land by the Canadian government,
  the railroad encouraged European immigrants by offering low cost travel packages
  to move to western Canada.  Immigrants travelled on a CP ship, then on CP train
  and bought farmland on the plains from CP for as little as $2.50 per acre.  Families
  could travel by train in a compartment car with beds and small kitchen.  See if
  you can build a successful railroad in western Canada and meet all the victory objectives.  The
  map is fairly large (21x21) and is intended to be played with 3 AIs.  Good Luck.
