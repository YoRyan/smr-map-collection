  A devastating hurricane destroyed the fishing and gambling industries at the Atlantic seaboard. You are given the assignment to restore the fishing, hotels and casinos. You have 6 years to complete.

  This is small 10 X 10 map. It should appeal of folks who like to build railroads. Money will be plentiful but to get the different goals you will have to rearrange the rails mid game unless you have a powerful enough computer. I try to keep the rail mileage under 4000 miles and the trains under 10. I wish there was a scenario condition to limit the trains and rail length.
