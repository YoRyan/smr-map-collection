  Develop the economy of California, from the Gold Rush to the growth of tourism in the 1880s.

  NOTES
  - This scenario uses 'slow time', so that each month takes three times as long as normal.  Engine maintenance costs are adjusted accordingly.
  - The smelter produces one load of gold per four loads of ore delivered.  All other industries produce just one load of output for every two loads of input.
  - Delivering passengers to a gold mine increase gold production (one additional load of ore per each load of passengers).
