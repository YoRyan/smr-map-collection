description: "A latecomer in railway construction, the Grand Duchy of Oldenburg built
  a network that made the most of a thinly settled, economically weak country.\n\tWhen
  economic development finally picked up toward the end of the nineteenth century,
  however, the railroad was there to allow farmers access to markets.\n\n*Special
  Rules*\n\tOn this map, everything is much slower than you may expect.  Industries
  produce at a leisurely pace, and depots take longer to put together a consist and
  load a train.  Upgrading stations is more expensive than usual.\n\tFor that reason,
  it may make sense to leave a train's consist intact over several local stops, even
  if the cars will remain empty part of the way.  You save valuable time by changing
  consists only at stations or terminals, if at all.\n\tTo boost production of swine
  and livestock, supply these farms with grain.  Post offices, court houses, and the
  government ministries in the capital city of Oldenburg will produce mail when supplied
  with passengers."
