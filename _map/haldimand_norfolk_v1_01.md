  The sandy, forested lowlands on the northern shore of Lake Erie were inhabited by the indigenous Huron and Neutral peoples when French explorers arrived in the 17th century, followed after the American Revolution by English settlers and Loyalist refugees. It is now 1849, and the colonial government of Canada West has passed a Railway Guarantee Act to guarantee loan interest on the construction of new railroads not less than 75 miles in length.
  American border traffic, raw materials, and international freight are all in dire need of transport. A building boom is on the horizon. The only requirement to qualify for the guarantee is that new roads must be built to something called Provincial Gauge

  This scenario is meant to be played with no AI competitors. It's also finicky; if it crashes while loading, try it a second or third time and it should work
