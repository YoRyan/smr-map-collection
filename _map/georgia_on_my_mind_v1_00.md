description: Welcome to Georgia On My Mind.  This map was designed as a single player
  SAM (Stand Alone Map).  There are no crossovers.  You must switch through to cross
  existing track.  The map runs in 3 Eras from 1940 to 2000.  Be aware that it takes
  2 loads of wood to create 1 load of lumber.  Spread your track out when you can.  Spaghetti
  track is your enemy.  You may find it advantageous to build industries where you
  need them.  You may also find times when removing existing trains can be helpful.  I
  hope that you enjoy the map.
